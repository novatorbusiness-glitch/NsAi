Don't count the salary — count the hour. A marketer, a copywriter, an analyst, and an admin run roughly 240,000 ₽ a month in salaries, about 343,000 ₽ with taxes. Over a year, that's close to 4.1M ₽. That's not an abstract number from a slide deck — it's the real cost of having someone write reports, process leads, and run the content plan while you sleep.

We break down why comparing "hire a person" to "turn on an AI team" is a question of arithmetic most people simply never run, not a question of technology.

## Routine isn't free — it just never shows up as a line item

A business owner who sets their own tasks, sends their own reminders, and does their own math isn't paying for that in cash directly — they're paying with time that isn't going toward growth. An hour spent on routine someone else should be handling is an hour that brings in no new clients and improves nothing about the product. The bill just never arrives as a single line at the end of the month, so it's easy not to notice.

## Why hiring is a risk, not an asset

The person you hire can get sick, burn out, or quit in three months — and then the whole hiring and onboarding process starts over. This isn't a criticism of people; it's a property of any system built around one person: it's fragile exactly where it depends on their mood and health. A team of three or four specialists isn't an asset that runs itself — it's a constant operational load on you as the manager.

## What changes when an AI team takes over the routine

AI agents don't get sick, don't quit, and are never "not in the mood" — they either work or they're stopped by an explicit decision. The economics are stark: agency unboxing is a one-time 19,900 ₽ payment (first month's subscription included), then 4,900 ₽ a month. Over a year that's roughly 79,000 ₽ against roughly 4.1M ₽ for a hired team — an order of magnitude cheaper for the same volume of routine: reports, content, initial lead handling.

The key difference isn't just cost — it's that a task set in the morning gets a result the same day, not a week of coordination and meetings later.

## Where a human is still genuinely needed

This isn't a story about firing everyone. An AI team takes over the routine — repeatable tasks with clear acceptance criteria. Strategic decisions, relationships with key clients, creative calls — still yours. The difference is you now spend your time on exactly that, instead of splitting it between strategy and writing this week's tenth social post.
