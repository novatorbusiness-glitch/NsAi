"ChatGPT writes badly" usually means the prompt had no structure. The model doesn't guess your funnel. It assembles text from whatever you fed it. Ask for "write me sales copy" and you'll get a template that sells to everyone and no one.

Feed the model a neuro-funnel structure, and the copy starts to land. We break down a prompt that assembles text by the rules: hook → pain → solution → offer → CTA.

## Why an unstructured model writes "into the void"

Without a brief, the model picks the most probable sequence of words. For the topic "sales," the most probable sequence is marketing clichés: "we're the best," "a unique methodology," "hurry up." That's not a model flaw — it's an absence of constraints.

A neuro-funnel is exactly that: a system of constraints. Each block owns one part of the brain and has its own job. Give the model those constraints, and it writes to them instead of to clichés.

## The prompt: five blocks

Build your prompt on this scaffold:

**1. Role and audience.** "You're a neuromarketing copywriter. You're writing for a small-business owner who's already tried advertising, but the leads aren't paying off."

**2. Hook (5 seconds).** "The headline names the pain and promises a result. Don't start with 'we.' Use an open loop and a concrete number."

**3. Pain.** "Describe the client's situation so they recognize themselves: what they feel, what they fear, what they've already tried. No blame."

**4. Solution and offer.** "Show the mechanism: why the problem happens and how the product removes it. Value before price, then the anchor. Defuse objections in advance."

**5. CTA.** "One next step, no pressure. Phrased as a benefit, not an order."

## Ready-made template

Copy this and fill in your own details:

```
Role: neuromarketing copywriter.
Audience: [who]. Their pain: [pain]. Product: [what it delivers]. Price: [how much]. Constraint: [why now].
Write a sales block for a landing page:
1. Hook — a headline with the pain and the result, an open loop.
2. Pain — the client recognizes themselves, 2–3 sentences.
3. Solution — the mechanism behind the problem, and the product.
4. Offer — value, price anchor, guarantee, a deadline with a reason.
5. CTA — one safe next step.
Write specifically, no clichés, in a natural voice.
```

## What you get on the other end

The model stops "advertising" and starts **speaking to the pain**. The copy becomes targeted: it's not "for everyone," it's for one specific person in one specific situation. That's the difference between a rewrite and copy that actually hooks the brain.
